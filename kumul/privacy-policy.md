# Kumul — Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 23 August 2026*

Kumul is a score-keeping app for card and board games. It works entirely offline and on your local network. The developer operates no server and receives no data from the app.

## Data the app processes

- **Match data** — game names, player names and scores you enter, stored only in the app's local database on your device.

## What is never done

- No data is collected, transmitted to, or stored by the developer.
- No analytics, no advertising, no tracking SDKs, no account.

## Live sharing (Premium)

If you enable live sharing, the app runs a small server on your device so other players *on the same local network* can follow the score from their phone, protected by a PIN. Nothing goes through the internet.

## Backup

Android's standard Auto Backup may include the app's database in your own Google account device backup, under Google's backup terms. You can disable this in Android settings.

## Data retention and deletion

Everything lives on your device: delete matches in the app, or uninstall it to remove all data.

## Children

Kumul stores no personal data beyond the names you choose to enter and is suitable for all ages.
